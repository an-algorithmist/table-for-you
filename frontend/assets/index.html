Source notebook

Research, with sources.

This agent searches accessible restaurant menus and customer-review passages. It translates dishes into English and separates supported matches from missing evidence.

History is stored in PostgreSQL for seven days after activity, and is currently tied to this browser. Account signup/signin is planned for the next stage.

Reviews are a selected sample. Menu prices and availability may change. Ingredient omissions do not establish absence, and cross-contamination is not verified.
